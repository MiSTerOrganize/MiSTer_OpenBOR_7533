## 4. CIRCUIT BREAKER REPORT (protocol §4)

### Root Cause — why the agents kept disagreeing

**The loop had no fixed point because the audit target was prose, and every fix pass
edited prose.**

The reviewers were instructed — correctly — to recompute from primary sources (pristine
v7533, `apply_patches.py`, the 450-PAK library, raw scan TSVs) and never from the
document's own claims. Under that instruction the **numeric spine re-derived EXACTLY in
every round**: all 20 band rows with zero overlaps, the whole bandwidth model, the M10K
and timing budgets, the px/clk chain, every RTL line citation. Seven rounds, zero
arithmetic defects in the spine.

What the rounds found instead was the ~2,000 lines of English wrapped around those
numbers — cross-references, enumeration counts restated in four places, labels, citation
line numbers, and narration of corrections. **Each fix pass rewrote prose, and rewritten
prose is new surface for the next reviewer.** Rounds 3, 5 and 6 all landed the identical
headline — *"the previous round narrated corrections it did not make, and one correction
it did make was itself wrong"* — which is the signature of a process that generates
defects at roughly the rate it removes them. The critical counts show exactly that:
**8 → 6 → 10 → 6 → 5 → 3**, with a rise at round 3 and no term approaching zero.

The compounding error was **conflating two different lists**:

- the **review findings** — prose defects, self-inflicted, unbounded; and
- the **register at §14.2** — the real engineering backlog, 22 items, each of which
  *"changes the RTL if answered wrong"*.

Seven rounds of review moved **one** register item (item 0, and only because someone
built a probe and measured it). An eighth round would have found more prose defects and
moved zero. The loop was not converging on the design; it was converging on the document
about the design, and those are not the same object.

### Delta — the exact remaining diff preventing approval

Two items, and only one of them is real:

1. **§6's memory map has no BUF2 row.** A third framebuffer is *mandatory* in three
   places (`:211` target-index width, `:1268` the throughput argument, §9.11.1 C1, and
   register 7a) and has no address space. `:1268` says *"Therefore: three framebuffers"*;
   §6 lists `BUF0/BUF1` at `0x3A000040 / 0x3A040040` and stops.

   **Not independently blocking.** §6 already states that *every base address in the
   table is provisional* pending register item 9 (M11's enumeration of existing DDR3
   consumers — `ascal`'s `vbuf` at `RAMBASE 0x20000000`, `ddr_svc`/`ram2`, the legacy
   `ddram` master, the `0x3A000000` window). BUF2 cannot be placed before that
   enumeration, so it is a *dependent* of item 9, not a defect beside it. **Folded into
   register item 9 rather than fixed now** — placing it now would be inventing an address
   the enumeration may contradict.

2. **The §9.11.5 enumeration says three where the canonical list says eleven.** Accepted
   under §3.2. The canonical list is right; the restatements lag.

**There is no diff that blocks anything, because nothing downstream is waiting on the
document.** Phase 2 is ARM-side only and gated on the register, not on the prose.
